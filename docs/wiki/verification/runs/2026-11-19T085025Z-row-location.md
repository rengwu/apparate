## Behavior and geometry

General now has its own **Location in results** card with a native Inline / Right / Below segmented control. The version-1 `resultLocation` key defaults to `right` when absent, accepts those three values, and rejects unknown choices, null and wrong types. It saves immediately through an expected-value transaction. Unrelated external edits survive; stale choices and failed writes preserve the persisted value. Broken config disables the control; external valid edits refresh it and the open launcher.

| Layout | Location | Row height | Panel heights: strip / 1 / 3 / 9 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inline | 12 pt, after a 10 pt gap | 28 pt | 41 / 110 / 166 / 334 pt |
| Right (default) | 12 pt, right-aligned 9 pt before the hint, at least 16 pt after title | 28 pt | 41 / 110 / 166 / 334 pt |
| Below | 11 pt, second line; location-free commands center their title | 40 pt | 41 / 122 / 202 / 442 pt |

No em dash is inserted. Inline/Right give the title priority: location text middle-truncates, then drops when less than a useful 32-point fragment remains. The title tail-truncates only after location space is gone. Below independently truncates title and location. Matched graphemes stay semibold, settings retain their pane location, full labels remain in accessibility, and selection colors are unchanged.

Layout-only changes do not rerun search or change its revision, selection, viewport, composition or pending actions. During a pending query, the previous complete rows/footer reflow immediately with their text intact, including a previous Restart confirmation subtitle. One row-height value drives drawing, invalidation, hit testing, wheel accumulation and AX frames. A height change resets fractional wheel remainder. The panel keeps its top edge; strip/status stay 41/82 points. Search/footer centering checks still pass in every mode.
